09 / 10Delivery & objections

Start on shared cloud.
Keep it practical.

DEFAULT

Shared cloud.

Pay for usage.
New models arrive without
infrastructure work for your team.

Trade-off: shared queues and vendor limits.
STEADY, LARGE VOLUME

Dedicated capacity

Reserve throughput when utilisation justifies it.

You also pay for idle capacity.
STRICT DEPLOYMENT NEED

Private VPC / on-prem

Control the deployment and its data boundary.

Narrower model catalogue. Heaviest operations burden.

“Why not our own GPUs?”

Count hardware, utilisation and the operating team. Veo, Seedance and Kling cannot be self-hosted.

“What if GPU costs fall?”

In practice savings don't arrive: rental is up ~15%, new flagships launch higher. Fix rates for the year and review them together annually — your output per dollar grows.

Evidence · Assumptions behind the deployment choice+
  • The source’s self-hosted example uses an ~$285K 8×H100 server plus power, hosting and a 3–4-person team: ~$1.2M a year.
  • At full utilisation, it estimates ~$6 per raw 5s / 720p Wan 2.2 clip. Cloud examples are $0.35–0.44 on different models — Kling 3.0 / Wan 3.0. This is not a like-for-like quality benchmark.
  • Private deployment is limited by model availability, licence and hardware requirements. Open weights do not automatically permit a particular geography or commercial use.
  • Shared and dedicated frontier-model calls still go to model vendors. Deployment choice does not remove the need to check data routing.

GPU pricing context: the October 2026 analysis records rental up ~15% and Seedance 2.5 at ~50% above 2.0. Individual models can still get cheaper: Veo fell from $0.75 to $0.40 per second. Fewer tries per approved asset are the customer benefit; rates are reviewed together annually.
