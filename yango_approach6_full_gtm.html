Approach 6: Agent-as-a-Service

Full GTM playbook for Yango.Tech CIS. Every element mapped against the B2B SaaS GTM framework: ICP, positioning, UVP, pricing, sales motion, channels, funnel, team, and 90-day plan.

1 — Positioning & UVP

What we are

Yango.Tech deploys AI agents that perform specific jobs inside enterprise companies in Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. Not consulting. Not "AI strategy." Working agents that handle calls, answer employee questions, or process documents — 24/7, in Russian and Kazakh, with measurable results tied to payment.

"We deploy AI agents that do real work in your company — handling customer calls, answering employee questions, or processing documents. Live in 4-6 weeks. You pay only if the metrics improve."

Why this UVP works

  • No expertise claim — we don't pretend to be banking experts with zero B2B track record. We sell working technology + accountability for results
  • Outcome-tied — "pay only if metrics improve" removes the biggest objection ("you have no case studies"). Client risk = near zero
  • "Agent" not "project" — different mental model for the buyer. Not a scary AI transformation. A digital worker that costs $2K/mo instead of $1.5K/mo human, but works 24/7
  • Time-boxed — "4-6 weeks" makes it feel actionable, not a 12-month initiative
  • Local language — "Russian and Kazakh" signals local relevance. No global AI vendor offers Kazakh language support

3 — Agent Portfolio

🎧 Customer Service Agent

Handles inbound customer calls and chats. Resolves standard queries autonomously, routes complex ones to human operators with full context.

Tech: SpeechKit STT/TTS + YandexGPT + Integration API
Intents: 20-40 per vertical (configured, not coded)
Languages: Russian, Kazakh, Uzbek
Metric: % calls resolved without human
Target: 40-60% automation rate
PoC timeline: 4-6 weeks
Pricing: $0.50-1.50 per resolved interaction OR $3-8K/mo flat

🧠 Knowledge Agent

Answers employee questions from internal knowledge base — regulations, policies, procedures, HR rules, product specs. Replaces "asking the senior colleague."

Tech: YandexGPT + RAG pipeline + Document ingestion
Data sources: Confluence, SharePoint, PDF policies, internal wikis
Languages: Russian (primary), bilingual docs supported
Metric: Time to answer, accuracy rate, adoption rate
Target: 80%+ accuracy on trained corpus
PoC timeline: 2-3 weeks (fastest agent)
Pricing: $2-5K/mo (by user count or flat)

📄 Document Agent

Processes incoming documents — extracts data, classifies, validates, routes. KYC forms, applications, invoices, contracts.

Tech: Vision OCR + YandexGPT + Workflow engine
Doc types: Passports, IDs, applications, invoices
Languages: Russian, Kazakh, bilingual documents
Metric: Processing time reduction, error rate
Target: 70-90% auto-processing rate
PoC timeline: 3-4 weeks
Pricing: $0.20-0.80 per document OR $2-5K/mo flat
Phase 1 focus: Launch with Customer Service Agent + Knowledge Agent only. Document Agent adds complexity (OCR pipeline, document-specific training). Add in Phase 2 (month 4-6) when first two agents are proven.

4 — Sales Motion — Three Hypotheses

Hypothesis A: "Demo-first outbound"

Best for: Telecom and Retail

Core idea: Build a working demo agent BEFORE any outreach. Lead every conversation with "let me show you." No pitch decks. No capability presentations. Working software on screen in the first 5 minutes.

LinkedIn message To: VP Customer Operations, Kcell

"[Name], quick question — what % of your inbound calls are "check my balance" or "change my tariff"? At Yango.Tech we've built an AI agent that handles exactly these calls autonomously — in Russian and Kazakh. I can show you a working demo in 15 minutes with your actual call scenarios. Worth a look?"
1st meeting script (30 min) Min 0-5: "Thanks for the time. I won't show slides. Let me show you something real." → Live demo: call the agent, ask "хочу сменить тариф", watch it handle the conversation.

Min 5-10: "This is configured for [telecom] scenarios. It handles 25 standard intents. Let me show you [balance check, complaint routing, tariff comparison]."

Min 10-20: Discovery: "How many calls/day do you handle? What % are standard? What's your current automation rate? What's cost per call?" → Building ROI model live.

Min 20-25: "Based on what you told me — 50K calls/day, 60% standard, $1.20/call — if our agent handles even 30% of standard calls, that's $X/month savings. Here's how we'd validate this."

Min 25-30: Propose paid PoC: "4 weeks, one call type (e.g. balance inquiry), real traffic, $15-25K. If agent doesn't hit 30% automation — you don't pay."

Why this works: Telco and retail CTOs are engineers. They trust what they see, not what they hear. A working demo in the first meeting is 10x more powerful than any pitch deck. And the "you don't pay if it doesn't work" removes all remaining objections.

What you need: Working demo agent configured for 10-15 telecom intents (balance, tariff, complaint) + same for retail (order status, returns, delivery). 2-3 weeks to build before first outreach.

Hypothesis B: "Problem-led warm intro"

Best for: Banks (Tier 2-3)

Core idea: Banks don't respond to cold outreach from unknown B2B brands. Entry must be through warm introductions — from Yango leadership, from existing Yandex Cloud contacts, from industry events. Lead with the client's specific problem, not your product.

Intro request to connector "We're looking to connect with [Head of CC / CDO] at [ForteBank]. We've built AI agents that handle customer calls in Russian and Kazakh — and we're offering the first 3 banks a risk-free pilot. Can you make an intro?"
1st meeting script (45 min) Min 0-5: "Thank you [connector name] for the intro. I'll be brief — we build AI agents for customer service. But first, I'd love to understand your situation."

Min 5-20: Deep discovery: CC size, call volume, top call reasons, current IVR performance, pain points, digital transformation priorities, budget cycle timing.

Min 20-30: Map 2-3 problems to specific agents: "Based on what you said — your biggest pain is [X]. Here's specifically how our Customer Service Agent would handle it." Brief demo.

Min 30-40: ROI discussion: "If we automate just [specific call type], based on your volume, estimated annual savings = $Y."

Min 40-45: "We're offering the first 3 banks in KZ a risk-free pilot — 6 weeks, one use case, outcome-guaranteed. Can we scope this in a follow-up next week?"

Why this works: Banking deals require relationship trust + problem understanding. You can't cold-email a bank CTO. But "risk-free pilot, first 3 banks" creates urgency and exclusivity.

What you need: 2-3 warm connectors into KZ banking (Yango leadership? Yandex Cloud contacts? Board members?). Banking-configured demo (balance inquiry, card block, loan status).

Hypothesis C: "Land with Knowledge Agent, expand to CC"

Best for: Any vertical — fastest path to first revenue

Core idea: Knowledge Agent is the easiest sell: no telephony integration, no production risk, no compliance complexity. Use it as a door opener, prove you can deliver, then upsell Customer Service Agent (the big money).

LinkedIn message To: CTO / CDO, any enterprise

"[Name], we've built an AI agent that answers employee questions from your internal docs — regulations, policies, procedures. Works in Russian. Setup takes 2 weeks, no IT integration needed. We're offering 3 companies in KZ a free 2-week trial. Interested?"
1st meeting (20 min — deliberately short) Min 0-3: "Let me show you. I uploaded your company's public HR policy from your website. Watch:" → Ask agent a question about vacation policy → Agent answers correctly from the document.

Min 3-8: "Now imagine this with your internal documents — 500 regulations, product specs, compliance rules. Your employees get answers in 10 seconds instead of 30 minutes."

Min 8-15: "Setup: you give us access to your document repository. We configure the agent in 2 weeks. Your employees start using it. We measure adoption and accuracy."

Min 15-20: "Free 2-week trial for the first 3 companies. After that — $3-5K/month. Deal?"

Why this works: Lowest risk, fastest PoC, easiest "yes." CTO can approve $3-5K/mo without board approval. Once inside, you have relationship + trust to sell Customer Service Agent (10x revenue).

Risk: Low ACV ($3-5K/mo = $36-60K/yr). Need volume. And "GPT over documents" is easy to replicate. Must move to CC Agent upsell within 3-6 months or competitor replaces you.

My recommendation: Run all three hypotheses simultaneously for 60 days.

Hypothesis A (demo-first) → 10 telecom + retail targets
Hypothesis B (warm intro) → 3-5 banking targets
Hypothesis C (Knowledge Agent land) → 10 mixed targets

After 60 days, data will tell you which motion converts. Double down on the winner. Kill the losers. This is how you avoid "analysis paralysis" — test, don't guess.

Capabilities that must exist BEFORE first outreach

Non-negotiable prerequisites (build in Week 1-3):

1. Working demo agent — configured for telecom (5 intents: balance, tariff, complaint, roaming, payment) and retail (5 intents: order status, return, delivery, product info, complaint). Must handle a real phone call or chat in Russian. This is your entire sales weapon.

2. ROI calculator — input: call volume, avg handle time, cost per call, current automation %. Output: annual savings, payback period. Spreadsheet is fine. Must be on screen during first meeting.

3. PoC playbook — standardized document: week-by-week plan, success metrics (pre-agreed with client), data requirements, integration scope, team allocation. Same structure for every PoC, customized content.

4. One-pager — not a pitch deck. One page: what agents do, how fast, what it costs, who you are. PDF. Sent after first meeting, not before.

Without these four artifacts, do not start outbound. You will get meetings you can't convert.

Refactor before next project. This retro is mandatory, not optional. It's the difference between Approach 6 and Approach 3 (body shop).

10 — 90-Day Execution Plan

Week 1-2
Build demo agents. SA + ML contractor build two demo agents: telecom CS agent (5 intents, Russian) + generic Knowledge Agent (loads any documents). Test internally. Make it work on a real phone call or web chat. This is the single most important deliverable — without it, nothing else matters.

In parallel: AE builds target list: 15 telecom contacts, 15 retail contacts, 10 banking contacts in KZ. LinkedIn Sales Navigator + manual research. Identify trigger signals (hiring, transformation announcements).
Week 2-3
Build sales toolkit. ROI calculator (spreadsheet), PoC playbook (template), one-pager (PDF). AE + SA collaborate. Not polished — functional. Can iterate after first meetings.

In parallel: Map warm intros for banking targets. Ask Yango leadership: "Who do you know at ForteBank, Bank CenterCredit, Jusan?" Ask Yandex Cloud contacts for KZ referrals.
Week 3-6
Launch outbound. All three hypotheses simultaneously:
• Hypothesis A: 50 LinkedIn messages to telecom + retail targets with demo offer
• Hypothesis B: 3-5 warm intro requests for banking
• Hypothesis C: 30 LinkedIn messages offering free Knowledge Agent trial

Target: 8-12 first meetings booked by end of Week 6.
Week 6-8
Run first meetings. Iterate pitch. After every meeting — debrief: what worked? What question killed us? What objection did we not handle? Update pitch, demo, and ROI calc after every 3 meetings.

Target: 3-4 PoC proposals sent by end of Week 8. At least 1 signed PoC.
Week 8-12
Deliver first PoC. All hands on deck. SA + ML contractor deliver. AE manages client relationship. Weekly progress calls with client stakeholder. Document EVERYTHING — this becomes Case Study #1.

In parallel: Continue outbound for next wave. Pipeline should have 3-5 PoC proposals at various stages.

Week 12 milestone: First PoC completed with measurable results. First case study written. Second PoC starting. Decision on which hypothesis (A/B/C) performed best → double down.
Week 12+
First annual contract signed. Convert successful PoC → 12-month contract. Begin expansion conversation (2nd agent type).

Productization starts: SA extracts reusable modules from PoC 1-2. Intent templates, integration patterns, monitoring tools. PM role activates (part-time).

Revenue target by Month 3: $30-60K from PoCs. Not profitable yet — investment phase. Profitability target: Month 6-8.
